Detailed Description

Decorator that gives an employee a competency level.

The levels are ordered Intern, Junior, Senior, and an employee can do everything at or below their own level. The level needed depends on the state of the work unit: Ready needs at least Intern, Ongoing at least Junior and Escalated at least Senior (see EmployeeComponent::canAdvance()). A senior can therefore advance a unit in any of these states, but an intern only in Ready.

Design patterns
  • Decorator (ConcreteDecorator).
  • Template Method (ConcreteClass): adds its part to prepare, perform and finish.
  • Prototype (ConcretePrototype): clone() copies the decorator chain.
